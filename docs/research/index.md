# Research notes

Background research that informs the system but isn't itself a decision: observations of other
products, prototype findings, competitive or protocol surveys. Notes are **dated snapshots** —
they record what was true when they were written and are not kept current.

Filenames are `YYYYMMDD-short-name.md`; newest first below. Notes may be written in Chinese
where the source material is (unlike [`../decisions/`](../decisions/index.md), which stays in
English). When a note leads to an actual choice, record that choice as a decision and link the
note from it.
